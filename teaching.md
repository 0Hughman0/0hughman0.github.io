# Academic Resources

Below are some academic resources.

You are welcome to use these for your own learning, or as inspiration for your own teaching.

## An Introduction to Semiconductors

This course was originally taught as part of a summer school for students ages 16-17.

The purpose of the course is to give them a broad overview of the physics of semiconductors, how they can be used to create electronic devices 
and how these devices contribute to everyday life.

Some practical demonstrations are also included.

Lectures:

1. [Seminar 1 - Conductors]()
2. [Seminar 2 - P and N type Semiconductor v2]()
3. [Seminar 3 Semiconductor Devices]
4. [Seminar 4 - Binary, Logic Gates, Transistor Logic]
5. [Seminar 5 - Adding Circuits and Computers]
6. [Seminar 6 - The Future of Transistors, and 2D Materials]

## Semiconductor Nanophysics - Electronic Devices

These lectures were originally presented as guest lectures as part of the TUe Semiconductor Nanophysics Postgraduate course.

The course zips through the fundamental physics of electronic transport, into semiconductor physics, devices, and concludes with an 
outlook on transistor technology.
